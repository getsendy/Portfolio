Shelby Carpenter

I’m a product and developer marketing leader with scrappy roots in journalism and mountain guiding. Both fields taught me to go deep on the details, to be confident operating under pressure, and to solve problems that at first glance seem unsolvable. I use the technology I market and walk in the shoes of users every day. I’ve spent most of the last decade working at the intersection of AI, databases, and developer infrastructure, as an IC, a manager, and a manager of managers. I do my best work in ambiguous spaces where the problem isn’t fully defined and someone needs to find the through line.
